# Scenario 1: No Pivot — Stagflation Lock-in

## Core thesis
Administration maintains tariffs despite record-low economic approval. GOP approval stays above the defection threshold. Dual supply shock (tariffs + Iran oil) persists. The Fed is trapped. Market grinds lower via rotation, not crash.

## Relationship to other scenarios
- S1 is the default if S2's defection math doesn't reach critical mass AND S3's ceasefire doesn't materialize
- S1 strengthens when S2's weakening conditions are met (defections stall, admin doubles down)
- S1 weakens when either S2 or S3 gains probability

## Strengthen conditions (check each — count how many are TRUE)

- [ ] Iran war continues without resolution or ceasefire talks stall
- [ ] Oil stays elevated (WTI > $90)
- [ ] Fed holds rates — neither cuts nor hikes
- [ ] No additional House Rs vote against tariffs beyond the original 6
- [ ] Consumer sentiment continues declining
- [ ] Earnings revisions negative for import-exposed sectors

## Weaken conditions (check each — count how many are TRUE)
- [ ] GOP approval drops below 78%
- [ ] More than 6 House Rs publicly break on tariffs
- [ ] Iran ceasefire materializes or Hormuz reopens
- [ ] Oil drops below $80
- [ ] Admin signals flexibility on tariff exemptions
- [ ] Must-pass legislation creates horse-trading opportunity

## Positioning framework
| Asset | Direction | Rationale |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Rates | Curve steepens | Front end anchored (Fed paralyzed), long end reprices on term premium + fiscal stress |
| Gold | Structural bid | Negative real rates + CB buying (585t/qtr) + de-dollarization + no policy relief |
| Equity vol | Elevated floor | Record put demand supports VIX floor at 20-25. Dispersion opportunity (single-stock vs index) |
| Sectors | Defensive | Healthcare, utilities, domestic software outperform. Import-exposed underperform. |

**Key trade expressions:**
- Steepener: 2s10s or 2s30s. Short TLT / long SOFR futures.
- Gold: Long GLD/futures. Amplify via GDX on margin expansion.
- Vol: Sell near-term vol, buy 3-6M tails. Long single-stock, short index (dispersion).
- Stress level: 10Y above 5% = regime shift.

## Elaboration template
When writing the S1 elaboration, follow this EXACT structure. Each section should be 1-2 paragraphs.

**1. Premise and current conditions**
State the core thesis. Cite the current readings (econ approval, GOP approval, oil price) and explain why the no-pivot outcome is plausible given these numbers. Reference the threshold table — how far is GOP approval from the defection danger zone?

**2. Rates — curve steepener thesis**
Explain the Fed's impossible position (can't cut due to inflation, can't hike due to growth). Explain why the long end reprices higher (SCOTUS tariff ruling → fiscal deficit → more Treasury issuance → term premium). Name the specific trade expressions.

**3. Gold — structural bid**
Explain why this is gold's ideal regime. Reference central bank buying tonnage, de-dollarization flows, real rate dynamics. Address the key risk: forced liquidation during cash-conversion deleveraging (cite BofA FMS crowded trade data if available). Frame dip-buying strategy.

**4. Equity vol — elevated floor with dispersion**
Explain the VIX floor mechanism (record put demand → feedback loop). Cite the surface vs depth divergence (index drawdown vs average member drawdown). Explain the dispersion trade opportunity. Name the structure (sell near-term, buy tails).

**5. Meta-point and invalidation**
State the meta-insight: grind, not crash — favors carry-and-hedge over directional. List the 3-4 key signals that would invalidate this scenario.
