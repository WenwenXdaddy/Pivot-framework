# Scenario 2: GOP Defections → Selective Tariff Rollback (BASE CASE)

## Core thesis
Republican defections reach critical mass, forcing selective tariff rollback via must-pass legislation (OBBBA or Section 122 negotiation). Partial rollback — baseline tariffs exempted for consumer goods, auto parts, agricultural inputs. Section 301 China tariffs and sector-specific steel/aluminum survive. Effective rate drops from ~17.5% to ~8-10%.

## Relationship to other scenarios
- S2 is the base case — the transmission mechanism completing as designed
- S2 breaks if S3 materializes (ceasefire removes political pressure, defection math stalls)
- S2 also breaks if admin successfully intimidates rebel Rs (S1 persists)
- The existing infrastructure: 6 House Rs already voted against Canada tariffs, 3 Rs broke procedural shield, SCOTUS struck down IEEPA tariffs

## Cross-asset implications
- **USD**: Weakens. Exceptionalism premium deflates. Import volumes = current account pressure.
- **Rates**: Mixed. Inflation expectations ease (front end positive). Growth recovery complicates Fed cut path.
- **Gold**: Risk-off unwind. Stagflation premium compresses. Corrects 5-10% but structural CB bid limits downside.
