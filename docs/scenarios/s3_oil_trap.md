# Scenario 3: Oil Drop → Approval Bounce → No Structural Pivot (THE TRAP)

## Core thesis
Iran ceasefire or Hormuz reopening removes gas price pressure. Approval bounces mechanically. GOP approval stabilizes above the 80% danger zone. The defection math from S2 no longer reaches critical mass. Admin claims vindication. Tariff status quo persists through midterms. This is the most underpriced outcome because market positioning is binary (crisis vs. pivot) and this scenario punishes BOTH sides.

## Relationship to other scenarios
- **S3 breaks S2's catalyst.** Without gas price pain, voters' acute affordability anger fades. GOP approval rebounds 4-6 points. Swing-district Rs no longer feel the heat to break ranks on tariffs. The entire S2 transmission mechanism is short-circuited at its input.
- **S3 also breaks S1's crisis thesis.** The stagflation premium compresses because one of the two supply shocks (oil) is removed. But tariffs remain — so you don't get the S2 relief rally either.
- **The result is a muddle:** moderate growth, sticky-but-not-crisis inflation, tariffs grinding on margins, the Fed in wait-and-see mode. The worst environment for directional bets.
