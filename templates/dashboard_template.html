Scenario 1

No pivot — stagflation lock-in

Premise
Approval at 29% econ / 82% GOP does not trigger Congressional revolt. Admin calculates that tariff reversal = base betrayal, and that midterm losses are survivable (Senate map favors GOP regardless). Policy stance maintained through Nov 2026.
↓
Macro
Dual supply shock persists. Tariffs + Iran oil disruption = sticky goods inflation + energy cost pass-through. Growth decelerates but doesn't crater. Fed trapped between inflation mandate and weakening labor data.
↓
Feedback
No relief valve for markets. Without tariff rollback, there's no catalyst for a broad risk-on rotation. Earnings estimates get revised down. Corporate margin compression accelerates in import-exposed sectors. The "wall of worry" market grinds lower via rotation and churn, not a monolithic crash.
Asset class positioning
Rates
Curve steepens
Front end anchored by a Fed that can't cut (inflation) or hike (growth). Long end reprices higher on term premium from fiscal stress + inflation expectations. 10Y pushed by deficit concerns as tariff revenue falls post-SCOTUS ruling.
Core: Steepener (2s10s or 2s30s)
Hedge: Short TLT / long SOFR futures
Key level: 10Y above 5% = stress regime
Gold
Structural bid
The ideal gold regime: negative real rates implied by stagflation, central bank buying at 585t/quarter, de-dollarization flows, and no credible policy relief. Path to $5,000+ consolidation well supported.
Core: Long gold (physical / GLD / futures)
Amplifier: Gold miners (GDX) on margin expansion
Risk: Forced liquidation in cash-conversion episodes
Equity vol
Elevated floor
VIX at 31 with record put demand creates a vol floor. Under the surface: avg Nasdaq member drawdown of 31% vs index 7%. Corrections via rotation and churn.
Core: Long vol via put spreads on SPX
Structure: Sell near-term / buy 3-6M tails
Dispersion: Long single-stock vs short index vol
Key analytical insight: Under Scenario 1, there's no single catalyst that resolves the uncertainty. It's a grind, not a crash. That favors carry-and-hedge strategies over directional bets. The steepener thesis is driven by the SCOTUS tariff ruling's fiscal implications — lost tariff revenue means more Treasury issuance, pushing term premium higher even as the Fed considers easing. Gold benefits from the ideal combination of negative real rates, central bank buying, and no credible policy relief valve.
Yield channel: Elevated long-end yields make the no-pivot regime more hostile to equities even without a fresh oil shock. A compressed or inverted ERP means investors are not being paid much for equity risk versus Treasuries, while a bear steepener keeps duration-sensitive sectors under pressure. The practical rotation is away from long-duration growth, housing, REITs, and levered cyclicals, and toward inflation hedges, quality cash-flow compounders, and curve-steepener expressions.

Scenario 2 — base case

GOP defections → selective tariff rollback

Catalyst
The defection infrastructure already exists. Six House Rs voted to overturn Canada tariffs (Feb 2026). Three Rs broke the procedural shield blocking tariff votes. SCOTUS struck down IEEPA tariffs. GOP approval at 82% and falling. The question isn't if — it's when the defection count hits critical mass.
↓
Vehicle
OBBBA reconciliation or Section 122 negotiation. Johnson admits "a challenge to find consensus on tariffs legislatively." But the razor-thin House majority means 3-5 Rs can extract tariff exemptions as the price of any must-pass legislation. Farm state Rs + swing district Rs form the coalition.
↓
Outcome
Partial rollback, not full reversal. Section 301 China tariffs + sector-specific steel/aluminum tariffs likely survive. What changes: the 10-15% baseline "Section 122" tariffs get exempted for key categories (consumer goods, auto parts, agricultural inputs). Effective tariff rate drops from ~17.5% to ~8-10%.
Rally anatomy — three phases
Phase 1
Sentiment snap
First credible signal of rollback. VIX compresses 5-8 points. Import-heavy names gap up 5-10% intraday. Short covering drives initial move. SPX reclaims 200-DMA.
Duration: 1-3 days
Phase 2
Earnings re-rate
Analysts revise margins. Wells Fargo estimates +2.4% S&P EBIT uplift from tariff removal. Consumer/industrial guidance lifts. Multiple expansion in tariff-relief basket (already +20% since SCOTUS hearing).
Duration: 4-8 weeks
Phase 3
Restocking cycle
Real economy response. Retailers and industrials rebuild depleted inventories. Import volumes surge. Transport and logistics benefit. Small caps re-rate on domestic demand improvement.
Duration: 2-4 quarters
Yield-amplified catalyst: If the 10Y yield stays in the 4.75-5.00% stress zone, tariffs are no longer the only affordability problem, but they become the most politically adjustable one. Selective rollback would be read as relief for both goods prices and financing stress, lifting REITs, homebuilders, housing-adjacent retail, regional banks, and high-quality cyclicals that have been discounted for higher-for-longer rates.

Scenario 3 — the trap

Oil drop → approval bounce → no structural pivot

Setup
Gas prices are the primary driver of approval erosion. Silver Bulletin attributes "much of the recent decline" directly to gas prices. The Iran war is compounding tariff-era affordability pressure — but it's the visible, pump-price inflation that voters react to fastest. Remove that input, and the political math changes.
↓
Trigger
Ceasefire or Hormuz reopening. Trump's 15-point plan is on the table. Allianz baseline: deal within 4 weeks, Brent ends 2026 at ~$70. EIA projects Brent dropping from $95+ to $70 by year-end if Strait reopens. Futures already price this: Dec 2026 WTI at $69 vs. spot ~$94.
↓
The trap
Approval recovers → pivot pressure evaporates → tariffs stay. If gas falls from $4+ back to $3, the most acute voter pain lifts. GOP approval stabilizes above the 80% danger zone. The defection math from Scenario 2 no longer reaches critical mass. The admin claims vindication: "We said oil would drop — and it did."
Sequence of events
Now
Brent $104, WTI $94. Gas $4+. Approval 29% econ. GOP at 82%.
Q2 2026
Ceasefire or Hormuz reopening. Oil drops 25-30%. Gas falls toward $3.
Summer
Approval bounces 4-6 pts. GOP stabilizes 84-86%. Defection pressure fades.
Nov 2026
Midterms with 10-15% baseline tariffs still in place. Slow-burn margin compression continues.
Why this is underpriced — market positioning is binary
What markets are pricing
Most positioning is a binary bet: either things get worse (long gold, long vol — Scenario 1) or a pivot happens (long tariff-relief basket, cyclicals — Scenario 2). The ceasefire trade is the popular relief bet. But relief from oil ≠ relief from tariffs. These are being conflated.
What markets are missing
The ceasefire removes the catalyst for both the bearish AND the bullish scenario. No oil shock = no stagflation crisis. But no oil shock also = no political pressure for tariff rollback. You're left in a muddle: moderate growth, sticky inflation, and tariffs grinding on margins — the worst environment for directional bets.
Oil-yield divergence: The trap is sharper when oil falls but yields do not. Gasoline relief can lift approval at the margin, while mortgage rates and credit costs keep the household affordability problem unresolved. That divergence argues for nuance in duration and bonds: ceasefire can compress risk premium, but persistent long-end yields limit the clean bull-steepening or broad duration-rally thesis.

Hurt

Positioning for the trap
Favored
Short vol / sell premium
Ceasefire compresses VIX from 31 to ~20. Vol risk premium has been historically wide during war. Mean-reversion kicks in hard. Sell straddles or iron condors on SPX.
Expression: Short VIX futures curve (contango normalises from backwardation)
Favored
Domestic-facing quality
Companies with pricing power, low import exposure, and domestic revenue bases. Healthcare, domestic software, regulated utilities. They don't need a tariff rollback to grow.
Expression: Long domestic quality factor / short tariff-relief basket
Fade
Tariff-relief anticipation
WF's basket already up 20% on SCOTUS anticipation. If political pressure evaporates with falling gas prices, these names are priced for a catalyst that doesn't arrive.
Expression: Reduce/hedge consumer import + apparel overweights
Nuanced
Gold — tactical short, structural long
Ceasefire unwinds war premium (-5 to -10%). But central bank buying (585t/qtr), fiscal deficits, and de-dollarization persist. The correction is a dip to buy, not a regime change.
Expression: Reduce tactical gold pre-ceasefire; rebuild on pullback to $4,500-4,700
Nuanced
Duration / bonds
Oil relief helps inflation expectations, but sticky term premium and fiscal supply can keep the long end heavy. Avoid treating ceasefire as an automatic all-clear for long-duration assets.
Expression: Prefer defined-risk duration longs or curve trades over unhedged TLT beta
Key analytical insight: JP Morgan analysts warn that even with a quick ceasefire, oil will face a lasting "security tax" from new shipping routes and higher insurance costs. Gas might fall from $4+ to $3.50, not back to $2.80. The relief may be less complete than markets expect — which means the approval bounce could be smaller than assumed, creating an uncomfortable middle ground where neither the crisis nor the relief thesis fully plays out.
