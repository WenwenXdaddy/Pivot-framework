# Scenario 1: No Pivot — Stagflation Lock-in

## Core thesis
Administration maintains tariffs despite record-low economic approval. GOP approval stays above the defection threshold. Dual supply shock (tariffs + Iran oil) persists. The Fed is trapped. Market grinds lower via rotation, not crash. Rising yields compress the equity risk premium toward zero, accelerating the rotation out of duration-sensitive assets.

## Relationship to other scenarios
- S1 is the default if S2's defection math doesn't reach critical mass AND S3's ceasefire doesn't materialize
- S1 strengthens when S2's weakening conditions are met (defections stall, admin doubles down)
- S1 weakens when either S2 or S3 gains probability
- **Yield linkage:** rising yields amplify S1's "slow grind" by mechanically reducing equity valuations. The bear steepener (long end rising faster than short end) is the signature rate regime of S1.

## Positioning framework
| Asset | Direction | Rationale |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Rates | Curve steepens | Front end anchored (Fed paralyzed), long end reprices on term premium + fiscal stress. **10Y above 5% = stress regime shift.** |
| Gold | Structural bid, but yield headwind | Negative real rates + CB buying (585t/qtr). **However: rising nominal yields and USD strength from safe-haven demand create tactical headwinds. Gold corrected 15% from $5,595 to $4,493 in part because of yield surge.** |
| Equity vol | Elevated floor | Record put demand supports VIX floor at 20-25. Dispersion opportunity (single-stock vs index). **ERP compression accelerates rotation into bonds, keeping vol bid.** |
| Duration equities | **Sell / underweight** | **Tech, REITs, utilities — all duration-sensitive. In 2022, the 10Y surge from 1.5% to 4.3% drove Nasdaq down 33%. Current 4.2% → 4.67% is a slower grind but from higher starting valuations.** |
| Value / short-duration | **Relative outperform** | **Banks benefit from wider NIM. Insurance benefits from higher reinvestment rates. Energy and commodities are the source of the inflation driving yields.** |

**Key trade expressions:**
- Steepener: 2s10s or 2s30s. Short TLT / long SOFR futures.
- Gold: Long GLD/futures. Amplify via GDX on margin expansion. **Buy dips below $4,350 support, but size smaller than pre-yield-surge given the nominal yield headwind.**
- Vol: Sell near-term vol, buy 3-6M tails. Long single-stock, short index (dispersion).
- **Duration rotation: underweight QQQ/XLK, overweight XLF/XLE. Track the ERP (earnings yield minus 10Y) as the mechanical trigger for rotation flows.**
- Stress level: 10Y above 5% = regime shift.
