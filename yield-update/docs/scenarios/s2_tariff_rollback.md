# Scenario 2: GOP Defections → Selective Tariff Rollback (BASE CASE)

## Core thesis
Republican defections reach critical mass, forcing selective tariff rollback via must-pass legislation. Partial rollback — baseline tariffs exempted for consumer goods, auto parts, agricultural inputs. Section 301 China tariffs survive. Effective rate drops from ~17.5% to ~8-10%. Rising yields create a SECOND catalyst for defections beyond gas prices — mortgage and borrowing cost pressure broadens the affordability pain to swing-district suburban voters.

## Relationship to other scenarios
- S2 is the base case — the transmission mechanism completing as designed
- S2 breaks if S3 materializes (ceasefire removes political pressure, defection math stalls)
- S2 also breaks if admin successfully intimidates rebel Rs (S1 persists)
- The existing infrastructure: 6 House Rs already voted against Canada tariffs, 3 Rs broke procedural shield, SCOTUS struck down IEEPA tariffs
- **Yield linkage:** rising yields ADD a second defection channel. Even if gas prices moderate, mortgage and borrowing costs hit suburban voters — the exact demographic swing-district Rs need. This makes S2 more resilient to S3's oil trap.

## Cross-asset implications
- **USD**: Weakens. Exceptionalism premium deflates. Import volumes = current account pressure.
- **Rates**: **10Y drops 30-50bp as tariff-driven inflation expectations ease. 2s10s spread compresses. This is the most powerful market response — the discount rate compression amplifies every other rally leg. But: the drop may stall above 4.0% if fiscal deficit and oil keep term premium elevated.**
- **Gold**: Risk-off unwind. Stagflation premium compresses. Corrects 5-10% but structural CB bid limits downside.
