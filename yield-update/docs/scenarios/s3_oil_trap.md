# Scenario 3: Oil Drop → Approval Bounce → No Structural Pivot (THE TRAP)

## Core thesis
Iran ceasefire or Hormuz reopening removes gas price pressure. Approval bounces mechanically. GOP approval stabilizes above the 80% danger zone. Admin claims vindication. Tariff status quo persists.

**YIELD COMPLICATION (major revision):** The original thesis assumed a ceasefire cleanly removes affordability pain. But yields may NOT normalize even if oil drops — tariff inflation, fiscal deficits, and the Fed's hawkish stance keep the 10Y elevated. If 10Y stays above 4.5% post-ceasefire, Channel 2 (mortgage/borrowing costs) keeps the affordability pressure alive, potentially undermining the approval bounce. This makes S3 LESS STABLE than originally modeled — the trap may spring open.

## Relationship to other scenarios
- **S3 breaks S2's catalyst** via Channel 1 (gas prices). Without gas pain, acute voter anger fades.
- **But S3 may NOT break Channel 2 (yields).** If tariff-driven inflation keeps yields elevated, mortgage and borrowing costs continue rising even as gas drops. The approval bounce from lower gas could be partially offset by rising mortgage rates.
- **New stability question:** S3 was originally modeled as a stable equilibrium (low vol grind). With yields staying elevated, it may be a transitional state that eventually tips into S2 (yield-driven defections) rather than a true trap.

## The oil-yield divergence indicator (NEW)

This is the key monitoring tool for S3's stability:

```
Oil-yield divergence = (% drop in WTI since ceasefire) - (bp drop in 10Y since ceasefire)

Interpretation:
  Oil down 25%, 10Y down 40bp  → Divergence small → Both channels easing → TRUE TRAP (S3 stable)
  Oil down 25%, 10Y down 10bp  → Divergence large → Only Channel 1 easing → UNSTABLE TRAP (transitions to S2)
  Oil down 25%, 10Y up         → Maximum divergence → Channel 2 intensifying → S3 breaks, S2 accelerates
```

Watch this ratio in the 2-4 weeks after any ceasefire announcement. The first week will be noisy (risk-off unwind). By week 3, the residual yield level tells you whether the trap holds.
